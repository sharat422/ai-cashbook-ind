# Backup and restore your data

**What it does:** Your entries are saved to your Bolcash account automatically, so you can get them back on a new phone.

**How backup works**
- There is no backup button: every entry is saved to your account when you are online.
- If you are offline, entries are kept on the phone and sync when you are back online. The Dashboard shows how many items are pending sync.

**Restore on a new phone**
1. Install Bolcash and log in with the same mobile number.
2. On the **Restore your data** screen, tap **⬇️ Restore my data**.

To restore later: open **Settings** (tap **⚙️** on the Dashboard) and tap **☁️ Restore data from cloud**.

**Limits**
- There is no backup file to download. To keep your own copy, export a PDF or Excel from **📊 Reports**.
- Don't uninstall the app while items are pending sync: they are only on that phone until they sync.
