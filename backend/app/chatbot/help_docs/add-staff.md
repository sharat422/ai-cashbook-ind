# Add staff or an accountant

**What it does:** Lets another person use your business in their own Bolcash app, with limited access.

**Steps**
1. On the Dashboard, tap **⚙️** to open **Settings**.
2. Tap **👥 Team & roles**.
3. Enter their **Mobile number** (10-digit).
4. Choose a **Role**: **Staff**, **Accountant** or **Owner**.
5. Tap **Add to business**.
6. They install Bolcash and log in with that mobile number (OTP). Your business appears for them.

**Roles**
- **Owner:** full access.
- **Accountant:** view + export + add/edit, no delete.
- **Staff:** add entries only (can't see balances, reports or customer lists).

To change someone's role or remove them, tap them in the team list.

**Limits**
- Only the owner sees **👥 Team & roles**.
