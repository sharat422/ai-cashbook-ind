# Add an entry

**What it does:** Records money coming in, money going out, credit given to a customer, or a payment received from a customer.

**Income (money received, e.g. a cash sale)**
1. On the Dashboard, tap **+ Income**.
2. Enter **Amount**, **Category** and **Date**. Notes and an attachment (receipt photo) are optional.
3. Tap **Save income**.

**Expense (money paid out)**
1. On the Dashboard, tap **− Expense**.
2. Enter the amount, **Category**, **Vendor / Paid to** and **Date**. Notes and an attachment (bill photo) are optional.
3. Tap **Save expense**.

**Credit to a customer (udhaar / maal diya)**
1. Open **👥 Customers** and tap the customer.
2. Tap **Add credit**.
3. Enter **Credit amount** and **Date**. Invoice number, notes and an attachment are optional.
4. Tap **Add credit**, or **Save as draft** to finish later.

**Payment from a customer (paisa mila)**
1. Open **👥 Customers** and tap the customer.
2. Tap **Receive payment**, enter the amount and choose the payment method (for example Cash or UPI).
3. Save it.

**By voice or typing a sentence**
1. On the Dashboard, tap **🎤 AI Entry (speak or type)**.
2. Speak or type, e.g. "Ramesh ko 2500 ka maal diya".
3. Check the details shown, edit if needed, then confirm to save. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

**Limits**
- The date can't be in the future. The amount must be more than ₹0.
- Income notes can be up to 280 characters; expense notes up to 5,000.
- If you are offline, the entry is saved on the phone and syncs when you are back online.
- Saved entries can't be edited or deleted yet (see "Delete an entry").
