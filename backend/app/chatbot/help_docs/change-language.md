# Change the app language

**What it does:** Changes the language of the app's text, and separately the language used for voice entry.

**App text**
1. On the Dashboard, tap **⚙️** to open **Settings**.
2. Under **Preferences**, tap **Content language**.
3. Pick a language: English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், ಕನ್ನಡ, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, বাংলা, മലയാളം or ਪੰਜਾਬੀ.

**Voice entry**
1. In **Settings → Preferences**, tap **Voice entry language**.
2. Pick the language you and your customers speak, for the most accurate voice entry.

**Limits**
- A few labels in the app are still shown in English.
