# Add a customer

**What it does:** Saves a customer (party) so you can track the credit (udhaar) you give them, the payments they make, and their outstanding balance.

**Steps**
1. On the Dashboard, tap **👥 Customers**.
2. Tap **+ Add** (or **+ Add customer** if you have no customers yet).
3. Enter **Full name** and **Mobile number** (10-digit).
4. Optional: **Business name**, **GST number**, **Address**, notes.
5. Tap **Add customer**.

To change details later: open the customer and tap **✏️ Edit**, then **Save changes**.

**Limits**
- The mobile number must be a valid 10-digit Indian number, and two customers in one business can't share the same number.
- Customers created by voice in **🎤 AI Entry** can be saved by name only; add their mobile number later with **✏️ Edit**.
- Customers can't be deleted in the app.
