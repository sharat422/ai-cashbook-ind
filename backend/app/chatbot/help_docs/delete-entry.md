# Delete or edit an entry

**What it does:** Bolcash can't delete or edit a saved entry yet. This applies to income, expenses, credit and payments. Customers can't be deleted either.

**If an entry was saved by mistake, contact support**
1. On the Dashboard, tap **⚙️** to open **Settings**.
2. Tap **💬 Help & support**.
3. Tap **💬 Chat on WhatsApp**, **🐞 Report a problem** or **✉️ Email us**, and tell us which entry is wrong.

**What you can do yourself**
- Before saving, tap **Cancel** to throw an entry away. For credit, **Save as draft** lets you finish it later.
- A customer's details (name, mobile, address) can be changed: open the customer and tap **✏️ Edit**.

**Limits**
- Deleting and editing saved entries is not available in the app yet.
