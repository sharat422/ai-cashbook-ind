# Send a payment reminder

**What it does:** Prepares a polite reminder message about the customer's outstanding balance and hands it to WhatsApp or SMS on your phone.

**Steps**
1. Open **👥 Customers** and tap the customer.
2. Tap **🔔 Reminder**.
3. Under **Template**, pick a message. You can edit the template text.
4. Choose how to send: **WhatsApp**, **SMS** or **Push**.
5. Check the **Preview**, then tap the send button (for example **Send WhatsApp reminder**).
6. WhatsApp or your SMS app opens with the message filled in. Press send there.

Past reminders for that customer are listed under **Reminder history** in the same sheet.

**Limits**
- Bolcash doesn't send the message by itself: you press send in WhatsApp or SMS.
- The customer needs a mobile number saved (add it with **✏️ Edit** on the customer).
- If WhatsApp isn't installed, the SMS app opens instead.
- **Push** sends a notification to you (your Bolcash notifications), as a note to yourself. The customer does not receive it.
