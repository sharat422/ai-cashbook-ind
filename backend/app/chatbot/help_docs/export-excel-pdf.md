# Export to Excel or PDF

**What it does:** Creates a PDF (to print or share) or an Excel file (to open in a spreadsheet) of your business report or of one customer's statement.

**Business report (profit & loss + all income and expenses)**
1. On the Dashboard, tap **📊 Reports**.
2. Choose the range: **Today**, **Week**, **Month** or **Quarter**.
3. Tap **⬇ PDF** or **⬇ Excel (.xlsx)**, then choose where to save or share it.

**One customer's statement**
1. Open **👥 Customers** and tap the customer.
2. Tap **📄 Statement** and choose the **Date range**.
3. Under **Export**, tap **📄 PDF**, **📊 Excel** or **🟢 WhatsApp**.

**Limits**
- The business report covers income and expenses. Customer credit and payments are in each customer's statement.
- Staff accounts can't open Reports; the owner or an accountant can.
