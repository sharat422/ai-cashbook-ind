3. How we use your data

Under the DPDP Act, we process your data on the basis of your consent, given when you sign up and accept this policy, and as necessary to provide the service you asked for (your cashbook).

  • Consent is specific to the purposes in §2. If we add a materially new purpose, we will ask again.
  • You may withdraw consent at any time (see §8). Withdrawing consent for data essential to the service (e.g. your mobile number) means we can no longer provide the service, and we will delete your account per §7.

5. Storage & security

  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Our Android app blocks unencrypted connections entirely.
  • Your sign-in token is stored in your device's hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain); the optional app-lock PIN never leaves secure hardware.
  • Access to production data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • No security system is perfect. If we discover a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

6. Retention — how long we keep data

  • Active accounts: we keep your data for as long as your account is active, so your books stay complete.
  • After deletion: when you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we delete your personal data from production systems within 30 days. Backups roll off within 90 days.
  • Legal holds: we may keep limited records longer where tax, accounting, or other Indian law requires it — in that case we keep the minimum required and nothing more.
  • Anonymized analytics: crash/usage aggregates that cannot identify you may be kept longer.

7. Account deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time: Settings → Danger zone → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. Deletion is immediate on our servers and irreversible. This satisfies both Play Store policy and your DPDP Act right to erasure.

8. Your rights (DPDP Act)

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  1. Access — get a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  2. Correction — fix inaccurate or incomplete data (most of it you can edit directly in the app).
  3. Erasure — delete your account and data (§7), unless retention is required by law (§6).
  4. Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (§1); if unresolved, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
  5. Nominate — nominate another person to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity, as the Act provides.

To exercise rights 1–3 or 5, contact sharatreddy730@gmail or use the in-app Help → Contact support. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's data

BolCash is a business tool and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will notify you in the app and/or by SMS at least 15 days before the change takes effect, and we will seek fresh consent where the law requires it. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.
